Planning & Vision

Camps built by people who live in them.

Mitchell has run drilling crews in remote locations for more than 55 years — building, supplying and living in the camps that support them. Foldable and modular buildings are widely available. What isn't widely available is a supplier who understands what happens to them after they leave the yard: the border crossing, the corrugated road, the wet season, the crew that has to sleep in them for the next eighteen months.

  • 01
    Operators, not just suppliers We specify the same accommodation standard we use for our own crews. If a fitting doesn't survive a Mitchell site, it doesn't go into a Mitchell camp.
  • 02
    In-country presence Established operations, logistics and customs experience across our countries of operation, supported from Australia.
  • 03
    Whole-of-camp capability Accommodation is the starting point — the same package extends to ablutions, kitchen and dining, power, water treatment, wastewater and full camp management.
  • 04
    Group backing Part of the Mitchell Group, privately held and operating 30+ rigs internationally, in continuous operation for over 55 years.
Camp Service Manifest

A full-service camp, the way Tier 1 operators expect it.

Mitchell Camps runs the whole camp — not just the tents. Every deployment carries the same manifest of services, scaled to the size of the site.

SVC-01 / DESIGN & CONSTRUCTION

Build

Camp design and construction to client brief — from standard containerised structures to clear-span container domes for mess halls, stores and workshops — with rapid mobilisation suited to the terrain.

SVC-02 / HOSPITALITY & HOUSEKEEPING

Live

Catering, housekeeping, laundry and day-to-day maintenance — run on-site, so crews get a home base whatever the roster.

SVC-03 / WELFARE & SECURITY

Protect

Recreation and common areas, on-site medical and first-aid provision, perimeter security and access control, and fatigue management for shift crews.

SVC-04 / UTILITIES & WASTE

Sustain

Self-sustaining hybrid power — solar, battery and diesel — plus potable water treatment and engineered wastewater systems, purpose-built for each camp class — our most detailed engineering discipline.

SVC-05 / WORKFORCE LOGISTICS

Move

Rostering, mobilisation and transport coordination, with digital check-in and muster accounting for fast, accurate evacuation if it's ever needed.

SVC-06 / COMPLIANCE & STANDARDS

Certify

Built to the health, safety and welfare standards Tier 1 clients audit against, with low-impact design and environmental compliance built in from layout stage.

Three continents, two engineering briefs

Same discipline. Different physics.

Africa and Australia demand heat and dust management; Chile's high Andes demand insulation, seismic resilience and altitude protocol. The camp class stays the same — the engineering underneath doesn't.

Hot / Arid

Africa & Australia

Kalahari · Bushveld · Outback exploration ground
  • Lightweight, off-road trailer builds — towable by a single LandCruiser
  • Solar-assisted power with generator backup for continuous 24-hr shifts
  • Evaporation-trench & drip-irrigation effluent disposal for water-scarce sites
  • Dust and heat-load management built into structure and siting, from the bushveld to the outback
Cold / High-Altitude

Andes, Chile

Atacama & Andean cordillera exploration ground
  • Insulated, seismic-rated modular structures for cordillera conditions
  • Heating load and freeze-protected water and waste systems in place of cooling
  • Altitude and acclimatisation protocol built into camp welfare planning
  • Logistics built around remote high-desert resupply and access roads
